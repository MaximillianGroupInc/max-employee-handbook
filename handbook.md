## 2. Employment Relationship

### 2.1 At-Will Employment

**Employment at MaximillianGroup is at-will.** This means that either the employee or MaximillianGroup may end the employment relationship at any time, for any reason that is not prohibited by law, with or without notice.

Nothing in this handbook, any offer letter, or any verbal or written communication from any MaximillianGroup representative creates a contract of employment for any specific period of time or changes the at-will nature of employment, unless a written employment contract signed by both parties expressly states otherwise.

This at-will policy may not be modified by any supervisor, manager, or representative of MaximillianGroup unless such modification is in writing and signed by the company principal.

---

### 2.2 Employee Classifications

MaximillianGroup classifies its workers as follows to ensure compliance with applicable wage and hour laws:

**Exempt Employees**

Exempt employees are salaried employees who meet the legal tests for exemption from California's overtime and meal/rest break laws under the executive, administrative, professional, or other applicable exemption. Exempt employees are expected to work the hours necessary to fulfill their responsibilities.

**Non-Exempt Employees**

Non-exempt employees are entitled to all California wage and hour protections, including overtime pay, meal breaks, rest breaks, and accurate timekeeping. Non-exempt employees must accurately record all hours worked.

**Part-Time Employees**

Part-time employees are those regularly scheduled to work fewer than 40 hours per week. Part-time employees may be exempt or non-exempt depending on their role and compensation.

**Temporary Employees**

Temporary employees are hired for a defined period or project. Temporary employment ends when the project or period concludes. Temporary employees do not receive ongoing benefits unless specifically provided in their offer.

**Seasonal Employees**

Seasonal employees are hired for recurring but time-limited periods tied to business cycles or seasonal demand.

*Classification as exempt or non-exempt is determined by California law, not by job title or the preferences of either party.*

---

### 2.3 Independent Contractors

MaximillianGroup works with independent contractors on a regular basis. This section applies to anyone engaged by MaximillianGroup as an independent contractor.

**Contractors Are Not Employees.** Independent contractors are not employees of MaximillianGroup. They are not entitled to employee benefits, workers' compensation coverage (unless separately arranged), unemployment insurance, or other employment-related protections unless required by law.

**Separate Contractor Agreements Required.** All independent contractor engagements must be governed by a written Independent Contractor Agreement signed by both parties prior to the commencement of any work. No independent contractor engagement is authorized without an executed agreement.

**California AB 5 and Borello Standards.** California applies a strict legal test — the "ABC test" under AB 5 (Labor Code § 2750.3 et seq.) — to determine whether a worker is an employee or an independent contractor for most purposes. MaximillianGroup takes worker classification seriously and is committed to lawful classification. Workers who believe they have been misclassified should raise the concern immediately.

For occupations potentially exempt from AB 5, the Borello multi-factor test may apply. Contractor classification will be reviewed with legal counsel as appropriate.

**Taxes and Insurance.** Independent contractors are responsible for their own federal and state income taxes, self-employment taxes, and any required business insurance, unless otherwise specified in the contractor agreement.

**No Implied Employment.** Contractors should not represent themselves as employees of MaximillianGroup without express authorization. Contractors must not sign contracts, make commitments, or incur obligations on behalf of MaximillianGroup without authorization.

---

### 2.4 Introductory Period

New employees may be subject to an introductory period of up to 90 days from their date of hire. The introductory period is not a guarantee of employment for any specific period; employment remains at-will throughout and after the introductory period.

During the introductory period, new employees and the company have the opportunity to assess fit, performance expectations, and role alignment. Feedback will be provided and expected. Completion of the introductory period does not alter the at-will employment relationship.

### 4.2 Anti-Harassment & Anti-Discrimination

**Policy Statement**

MaximillianGroup is committed to maintaining a workplace free from unlawful harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, vendors, and clients in all work-related contexts.

**Prohibited Conduct**

Harassment is unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic that creates a hostile, intimidating, or offensive work environment, or that results in an adverse employment decision. Harassment may be verbal, written, physical, visual, or electronic. Examples include, but are not limited to:

- Offensive jokes, slurs, epithets, or name-calling
- Threats, intimidation, or coercion
- Unwelcome physical contact
- Displaying or circulating offensive materials
- Sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, or other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature (sexual harassment)
- Interference with work performance based on a protected characteristic

**California FEHA Rights**

Employees have rights under the California Fair Employment and Housing Act (FEHA) to a workplace free from discrimination and harassment. FEHA covers employers with five or more employees for most harassment claims and two or more employees for sexual harassment. MaximillianGroup complies with FEHA regardless of its current employee count.

**Reporting Procedures**

Any employee who believes they have experienced or witnessed harassment, discrimination, or retaliation should report it immediately to the company principal. Reports may be made verbally or in writing.

If the company principal is the subject of the complaint, or if the employee is uncomfortable reporting to the company principal, the employee may report directly to MaximillianGroup's legal counsel, or file a complaint directly with:

- **California Civil Rights Department (CRD):** 1-800-884-1684 | https://calcivilrights.ca.gov
- **U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC):** 1-800-669-4000 | https://www.eeoc.gov

**Investigation**

MaximillianGroup will promptly and thoroughly investigate all complaints. Investigations will be conducted impartially and with appropriate confidentiality. Both parties will have an opportunity to present information.

**No Retaliation**

MaximillianGroup strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports harassment or discrimination in good faith, participates in an investigation, or exercises any legal right. Retaliation is independently subject to disciplinary action.

**Supervisor Obligations**

Anyone in a supervisory role at MaximillianGroup is required to:

- Model appropriate professional conduct
- Report any harassment or discrimination they observe or become aware of, even if no formal complaint is made
- Never condition any employment benefit on an employee's submission to or rejection of unwelcome conduct
- Cooperate fully in any investigation

### 4.3 Complaint Reporting Procedure

Employees who have a work-related concern, complaint, or grievance — including but not limited to concerns about harassment, discrimination, wage and hour compliance, safety, or other policy violations — are encouraged to raise the issue promptly.

**How to Report:**

1. Speak directly with the company principal.
2. Submit a written complaint to the company principal by email or the Employee Complaint Form (see Section 12).

**Response Process:**

MaximillianGroup will acknowledge receipt of the complaint, conduct an investigation appropriate to the nature of the complaint, and communicate findings and any resulting action to the extent permissible and appropriate. The timeframe for resolution will depend on the complexity of the matter.

## 5. Attendance, Scheduling & Remote Work

### 5.1 Work Schedules

MaximillianGroup does not maintain a single standard work schedule. Work schedules are established based on role, client needs, and individual arrangements. Non-exempt employees will have a defined schedule that is communicated at or before the start of employment.

Employees are expected to be available and responsive during agreed working hours and to communicate promptly about any changes to their availability.

---

### 5.2 Timekeeping

**Non-Exempt Employees.** All non-exempt employees are required to accurately record all time worked, including start and end times and all meal and rest break periods. Time records must be submitted in accordance with MaximillianGroup's payroll practices.

Falsification of time records is a serious violation of company policy and may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

**Exempt Employees.** Exempt employees are not required to track hours worked in the same manner, but may be asked to track time for project management or client billing purposes.

---

### 5.3 Meal & Rest Breaks

MaximillianGroup complies with California's mandatory meal and rest break requirements.

**Meal Breaks:**

- Non-exempt employees working more than 5 hours in a workday are entitled to an unpaid, uninterrupted meal period of at least 30 minutes before the end of the 5th hour of work.
- If an employee works more than 10 hours in a workday, a second meal period of at least 30 minutes is required before the end of the 10th hour.
- A first meal period may be waived by mutual written consent if the total hours worked is no more than 6 hours. A second meal period may be waived if the total hours worked is no more than 12 hours and the first meal period was not waived.

**Rest Breaks:**

- Non-exempt employees are entitled to a paid, uninterrupted rest break of at least 10 minutes for every 4 hours worked (or major fraction thereof).
- Rest breaks cannot be combined with meal breaks or taken at the beginning or end of a shift.

**Premium Pay.** If MaximillianGroup fails to provide a required meal or rest break, the employee is entitled to one additional hour of pay at the regular rate of compensation for each missed break, per California Labor Code § 226.7.

---

### 5.4 Overtime

MaximillianGroup complies with California's overtime laws, which are more generous than federal law.

**Daily Overtime:**

- Non-exempt employees are entitled to overtime pay at 1.5x their regular rate for hours worked over 8 in a single workday and for the first 8 hours worked on the 7th consecutive day in a workweek.
- Double time (2x regular rate) is required for hours worked over 12 in a single workday and for all hours over 8 on the 7th consecutive workday in a workweek.

**Weekly Overtime:**

- Non-exempt employees are entitled to overtime at 1.5x for hours worked over 40 in a workweek (in addition to daily overtime, whichever provides greater pay).

**Authorization.** Non-exempt employees should obtain advance approval before working overtime. Unauthorized overtime will be paid as required by law, but may be subject to disciplinary action.

---

Regular attendance and punctuality are essential to fulfilling professional obligations and client commitments.

Employees are expected to:

- Be present and ready to work at their scheduled start time
- Provide advance notice to the company principal of any expected absence or tardiness as soon as practicable
- Notify the company principal promptly if unexpected absence or tardiness arises
- Keep absences and tardiness to a minimum

Excessive or patterned absenteeism, tardiness, or failure to give notice may result in disciplinary action.

### 5.6 Remote Work Policy

MaximillianGroup supports remote and hybrid work arrangements. Many employees perform all or most of their work remotely.

**Home Office Expectations**

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a work environment that allows them to perform their duties effectively and professionally. This includes:

- A dedicated workspace that is reasonably free from distractions during working hours
- A reliable internet connection with adequate speed for video conferencing and file transfer
- Proper physical security for company and client materials and equipment

**Confidentiality**

Remote employees must take all reasonable steps to protect confidential company and client information. This includes:

- Not conducting confidential conversations in public or semi-public spaces
- Ensuring computer screens are not visible to unauthorized persons
- Securing physical documents
- Locking devices when not in use

**Internet and Security Requirements**

- Use company-approved security software, including VPN and antivirus tools as directed
- Do not conduct company work on public or unsecured Wi-Fi without a VPN
- Follow password management best practices; do not share credentials
- Report any security incidents, suspected breaches, or lost/stolen devices immediately

**Equipment Usage**

Company-provided equipment is for business use. Limited personal use that does not interfere with work performance is generally acceptable, but employees should have no expectation of privacy in their use of company equipment or systems.

Personal devices used for company work must comply with the same security requirements as company-owned devices.

**Zoom / Virtual Meeting Professionalism**

When participating in client-facing virtual meetings:

- Use a professional background or a clean, uncluttered physical setting
- Mute your microphone when not speaking
- Dress professionally for video-enabled sessions
- Test audio and video in advance of important meetings
- Be on time; pre-join a few minutes early for high-stakes calls

### 7.2 Paid Sick Leave

In compliance with California's Healthy Workplaces Healthy Families Act, all employees (including part-time and temporary employees) who work in California for at least 30 days in a year are entitled to paid sick leave.

**Accrual:** Employees accrue paid sick leave at a rate of at least 1 hour per 30 hours worked, or MaximillianGroup may provide a lump-sum amount at the beginning of the year consistent with California law.

**Use:** Employees may use accrued paid sick leave beginning on the 90th day of employment (or sooner if required by local ordinance). Paid sick leave may be used for:

- The employee's own illness, injury, or preventive care
- Care for a family member (as defined by California law)
- Absences related to domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking

**Carryover and Cap:** Unused paid sick leave carries over year to year, subject to applicable caps under California law.

**Notice:** Employees should provide as much advance notice as possible when taking planned sick leave. For unexpected illness, employees should notify the company principal as soon as practicable.

**Accrual Cap.** MaximillianGroup may impose a reasonable accrual cap, at which point employees temporarily stop accruing additional vacation until they use some of their accrued balance.

**Payout at Separation.** Accrued, unused vacation must be paid out at the final rate of pay upon separation from employment.

### 7.5 Leaves of Absence

MaximillianGroup complies with all applicable federal and California leave laws. Available leaves of absence depend on the company's current employee count and the specific circumstances. Employees should notify the company principal as early as possible when a leave of absence may be needed.

**California Family Rights Act (CFRA)**

Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year for qualifying reasons including birth or adoption of a child, care for a seriously ill family member, or the employee's own serious health condition. Eligibility and qualifying reasons are defined by California law. (Applicable to employers with 5+ employees.)

**Pregnancy Disability Leave (PDL)**

Employees disabled by pregnancy, childbirth, or related medical conditions are entitled to up to 4 months of leave under California's Pregnancy Disability Leave law, regardless of length of service or hours worked. (Applicable to employers with 5+ employees.)

**Jury Duty**

Employees summoned to jury duty are entitled to take time off to fulfill this obligation. MaximillianGroup will comply with California Labor Code § 230 regarding jury duty leave. Employees should provide advance notice as soon as they receive a summons.

**Voting Leave**

Employees who do not have sufficient time outside of working hours to vote may take up to 2 hours of paid time off at the beginning or end of their shift to vote, provided they give advance notice. (California Elections Code § 14000.)

**Military Leave**

MaximillianGroup provides military leave in accordance with the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA) and applicable California law.

**Bereavement Leave**

Employees may take up to 5 days of bereavement leave upon the death of a qualifying family member. Leave is unpaid for employers with fewer employees than required by law; paid leave may be provided at the company's discretion.

**Other Leaves**

Additional leaves may be available including, but not limited to: domestic violence/sexual assault leave, school activities leave, victims of crime leave, and reproductive loss leave (for employers with 5+ employees per SB 848). Employees should consult with the company principal about specific circumstances.

## 8. Technology, Confidentiality & Intellectual Property

### 8.1 Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information of MaximillianGroup and its clients, including but not limited to:

- Client identities, strategies, campaigns, marketing plans, and data
- Proprietary methods, processes, and systems
- Financial information, projections, and business plans
- Personnel information
- Vendor and partner relationships and terms
- Proposals, pitches, and creative concepts
- Trade secrets as defined by California law

Employees must protect Confidential Information during and after employment. Confidential Information may not be disclosed to any person outside MaximillianGroup without authorization, and may not be used for any purpose other than performing job duties.

Upon separation from employment, all Confidential Information — including documents, files, and data — must be returned or destroyed per Section 10.3.

---

### 8.2 Client Data Protection

MaximillianGroup and its employees are entrusted with client data and have a heightened obligation to protect it.

**Minimum Practices:**

- Access client data only as necessary for the performance of authorized work
- Store client data only in authorized, company-approved systems
- Do not download or store client data on personal devices without authorization
- Do not share client data with any third party (including subcontractors) without express authorization
- Report any potential data breach, unauthorized access, or mishandling of client data immediately to the company principal

MaximillianGroup complies with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) as applicable. Employees who have questions about data privacy obligations should consult with the company principal.

---

### 8.3 Intellectual Property Ownership

This section is critical for an advertising and marketing agency. All employees should read this section carefully.

**Work Product Ownership**

All work product, creative materials, strategies, deliverables, code, designs, and any other materials created by an employee in the course of employment — whether or not created during regular work hours, on company equipment, or on personal devices — is the sole property of MaximillianGroup (and/or its clients, as applicable per client agreements).

This includes:

- Advertising and marketing campaigns
- Creative assets (copy, design, photography direction, video concepts, brand elements)
- Business development materials, pitch decks, and proposals
- Program designs, frameworks, and methodologies
- Client deliverables of any kind
- AI-generated materials (see Section 8.6)

**California Labor Code § 2870 Limitation**

Pursuant to California Labor Code § 2870, MaximillianGroup does not claim ownership of inventions that an employee develops entirely on their own time, without use of company equipment, supplies, facilities, or trade secret information — unless the invention relates directly to MaximillianGroup's business or to MaximillianGroup's actual or anticipated research and development, or results from any work performed by the employee for MaximillianGroup.

**Assignment of Rights**

Employees agree, as a condition of employment, to assign to MaximillianGroup all right, title, and interest in and to all work product covered by this policy. Employees will execute any additional documents necessary to confirm such assignment upon request.

**Moral Rights Waiver**

To the extent permitted by law, employees waive any moral rights or rights of attribution in work product created in the course of employment.

### 8.4 Computer, Email & Internet Usage

MaximillianGroup provides and may authorize use of computers, email, internet access, and other technology systems for business purposes. Use of these systems is governed by this policy.

**Authorized Use**

Technology systems are provided primarily for business use. Limited, incidental personal use that does not interfere with job performance and does not consume significant company resources is generally permitted. However, employees have no right of privacy in their use of company systems, devices, or accounts.

**Prohibited Uses**

The following uses are strictly prohibited:

- Accessing, transmitting, or storing pornographic, obscene, or offensive material
- Harassment or intimidation through electronic means
- Unauthorized downloading or installation of software
- Circumventing security controls
- Using company systems for personal business, solicitation, or commercial gain without authorization
- Sharing, transmitting, or storing client confidential information in unsecured systems

**Monitoring**

MaximillianGroup reserves the right to monitor, access, retrieve, and review information stored, transmitted, or received using company-provided systems. Employees have no expectation of privacy in company systems. [If applicable: MaximillianGroup will provide notice of electronic monitoring as required by California law.]

### 8.5 Social Media Policy

**Company Accounts**

MaximillianGroup may maintain social media accounts for business purposes. Only authorized employees may post to official MaximillianGroup accounts. All content posted to company accounts must be reviewed and approved in accordance with MaximillianGroup's standard processes.

**Personal Accounts**

Employees may maintain personal social media accounts, but must:

- Not disclose Confidential Information, including client names and strategies, without authorization
- Not represent personal opinions as official MaximillianGroup positions
- Not post content that constitutes harassment, discrimination, or threats
- Not post content that could damage MaximillianGroup's or a client's reputation without a legitimate protected-activity purpose

California law protects employees' rights to discuss wages, working conditions, and other terms of employment. Nothing in this policy prohibits employees from exercising rights protected by the National Labor Relations Act or California law.

### 8.6 AI & Generative Tools Policy

MaximillianGroup recognizes that artificial intelligence and generative AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Midjourney, DALL-E, and similar tools) can be powerful components of creative and business workflows. We are committed to using AI responsibly, ethically, and in compliance with our legal obligations.

**Approved Use**

AI tools may be used for:

- Research, brainstorming, and ideation
- Draft generation for internal review (with appropriate human oversight and editing)
- Image or visual concept exploration (subject to client and IP restrictions)
- Data analysis and summarization of non-confidential materials

**Restrictions and Obligations**

Employees using AI tools must comply with the following:

1. **Client Confidentiality:** Do not input client confidential information, proprietary data, strategies, or identifying information into any third-party AI tool without express written authorization. This includes names of clients, campaign details, budgets, and strategic documents.

2. **Approval Before Delivery:** AI-generated content submitted to clients or published publicly must be reviewed, edited, and approved through MaximillianGroup's standard review process before delivery. No AI-generated material should be delivered to a client without human review.

3. **Copyright and Ownership:** The copyright status of AI-generated content is evolving under U.S. and California law. Employees should not assume AI-generated materials are copyright-protected or freely usable without legal analysis. When in doubt, consult the company principal before using AI-generated work in client deliverables.

4. **Accuracy:** AI tools may produce inaccurate, outdated, or misleading information. Employees are responsible for independently verifying facts, claims, and figures before including them in deliverables.

5. **Data Privacy:** Many AI tools store user inputs. Do not input personal information about employees, clients, consumers, or other individuals into AI tools unless the tool has been specifically approved for such use.

6. **Disclosure:** If a client agreement or request requires disclosure of AI use in deliverables, employees must comply with that requirement.

### 8.7 Recording Meetings & Communications

**California is a two-party (all-party) consent state.**

Under California Penal Code § 632, recording a confidential communication — including in-person conversations, telephone calls, and video calls — without the consent of all parties is a crime and may subject MaximillianGroup and the employee to significant civil liability.

**Requirements:**

- Employees must obtain the consent of all parties before recording any call, meeting, or conversation
- This applies to phone calls, video meetings, and in-person conversations
- Many video conferencing platforms display or announce a recording notification — this alone may not be sufficient for California compliance; explicit verbal or written consent from all participants is best practice
- Recordings made with consent must be stored securely and handled in accordance with the confidentiality obligations in this handbook

Do not record conversations involving clients, third parties, or colleagues without prior, unambiguous consent.

MaximillianGroup is committed to providing a safe and healthy work environment for all employees, whether working at a company office, client site, or home office.

Employees are expected to:

- Follow all applicable safety rules and procedures
- Report unsafe conditions, accidents, or near-misses to the company principal promptly
- Use equipment and tools safely and in accordance with manufacturer instructions

- Position the monitor at eye level and at a comfortable distance
- Keep wrists neutral when typing
- Take regular stretch breaks

MaximillianGroup provides workers' compensation insurance as required by California law. This insurance covers injuries or illnesses arising out of and in the course of employment.

If you are injured at work:

1. Seek necessary medical treatment immediately (call 911 for emergencies)
2. Report the injury to the company principal as soon as possible (within 24 hours when practicable)
3. Complete a workers' compensation claim form (DWC-1), which will be provided by the company

**No Retaliation.** MaximillianGroup will not retaliate against any employee for filing a legitimate workers' compensation claim.

## 10. Separation of Employment

### 10.1 Voluntary Resignation

Employees who wish to resign are encouraged, but not required, to provide advance written notice. While at-will employment means no notice is required, we ask for at least **two weeks' notice** as a professional courtesy to allow for an orderly transition.

Upon receiving notice of resignation, MaximillianGroup may, in its discretion, accelerate the separation date while paying out any remaining scheduled work period.

---

### 10.2 Final Pay Rules

California has strict rules regarding final pay timing:

| Separation Type | Final Pay Deadline |
|---|---|
| Involuntary termination (including layoff) | Immediately (same day) |
| Resignation with 72+ hours' notice | Last day of work |
| Resignation with less than 72 hours' notice | Within 72 hours of resignation |

Final pay must include all earned but unpaid wages, including all accrued, unused vacation (if a vacation policy exists). Failure to timely pay final wages may result in waiting time penalties under California Labor Code § 203 of up to 30 days of wages.

---

### 10.3 Return of Company Property

Upon separation, employees must promptly return all company property, including but not limited to:

- Company-owned computers, phones, tablets, or other devices
- Access cards, keys, and badges
- Company credit cards
- Client materials and files (physical and digital)
- Any other property belonging to MaximillianGroup or its clients

Unreturned company property may be subject to recovery through appropriate legal means. MaximillianGroup will not deduct the value of unreturned property from final wages without a written authorization signed by the employee (and only if such deduction would not reduce wages below minimum wage), except as permitted by California law.

---

### 10.4 Exit Procedures

At or before the last day of employment, the company principal will conduct or arrange:

- Revocation of access credentials (email, internal systems, client platforms)
- Transfer or transition of ongoing work responsibilities
- Completion of any required exit documentation
- Review of continuing obligations (see Section 10.5)

Former employees may request their personnel file in accordance with California Labor Code § 1198.5.

---

### 10.5 Continuing Confidentiality Obligations

The obligation to protect MaximillianGroup's and clients' Confidential Information survives the end of employment. Former employees may not:

- Use or disclose Confidential Information for any purpose after separation
- Retain copies of client files, strategies, or proprietary materials
- Contact MaximillianGroup's clients in ways that breach any applicable non-solicitation provision

Former employees will be reminded of these obligations in the exit process and may be asked to sign an acknowledgment.

*Note: California law generally does not enforce post-employment non-compete agreements. MaximillianGroup does not require employees to sign non-compete agreements, and any such provision will be construed consistent with California Business and Professions Code § 16600. Non-solicitation provisions remain subject to ongoing legal review.*

## 11. Required California Policies & Notices

### Paid Sick Leave Notice

MaximillianGroup provides paid sick leave to all eligible employees as required by the Healthy Workplaces Healthy Families Act (California Labor Code §§ 245–249). Employees who believe they have not received their entitled paid sick leave should contact the company principal or the California Labor Commissioner's Office.

### Harassment Policy (Summary)

MaximillianGroup prohibits harassment based on any protected characteristic. See Section 4.2 for the full policy. Complaints may be directed to the company principal or to the California Civil Rights Department (CRD) at 1-800-884-1684.

### Victims of Crime Leave

California Labor Code § 230.1 requires employers with 25 or more employees to provide leave for victims of domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking to seek relief, medical attention, or safety planning. MaximillianGroup provides this leave to the extent required by law and, to the extent legally permissible, on a voluntary basis regardless of employee count.

### Whistleblower Protections

California Labor Code § 1102.5 prohibits employers from retaliating against employees who disclose information they reasonably believe constitutes a violation of law. MaximillianGroup will not retaliate against any employee for making a good-faith complaint to a government agency, law enforcement, or to MaximillianGroup itself. The State of California maintains a whistleblower hotline at 1-800-952-5225.

### Paid Family Leave (PFL)

California's Paid Family Leave program is administered by the State of California through the Employment Development Department (EDD). Eligible employees may receive partial wage replacement benefits through the state program for qualifying family care leaves. This is a state program, not a MaximillianGroup benefit. For information, visit https://www.edd.ca.gov/disability/paid_family_leave.htm.

### Workers' Compensation Notice

MaximillianGroup provides workers' compensation insurance as required by California law. If you are injured on the job, notify the company principal immediately. You have the right to seek medical treatment and to file a claim for workers' compensation benefits.

### OSHA / Cal/OSHA Notice

Employees have rights under California Occupational Safety and Health regulations (Cal/OSHA). Information about Cal/OSHA is available at https://www.dir.ca.gov/dosh/. Employees have the right to report workplace hazards to Cal/OSHA without retaliation.

### Reproductive Loss Leave

California SB 848 (effective January 1, 2024) requires employers with 5 or more employees to provide up to 5 days of unpaid leave following a reproductive loss event (including miscarriage, failed surrogacy, and related events). MaximillianGroup complies with this law as applicable and will implement it voluntarily regardless of employee count.

### Electronic Monitoring Disclosures

If MaximillianGroup monitors or intercepts employee electronic communications or computer usage, employees will be notified as required by California Penal Code § 630.8 and applicable law. See also Section 8.4 (Computer, Email & Internet Usage).

### CCPA / Privacy Disclosures

Employees who are California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) regarding the collection, use, and sharing of their personal information. MaximillianGroup will provide separate privacy notices to employees as required by applicable law.
